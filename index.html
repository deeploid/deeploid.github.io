About

I am a Staff Research Scientist at Meta AI, where I lead data curation and pretraining for the open-source on-device MobileLLM and MobileMoE model series. My research focuses on data-centric training of foundation models: deciding what data a model should see, in what proportion, and in what order, so that a fixed compute budget buys the most capability.

I built the influence-based data-mixing framework used across text, multilingual, vision, and speech training at Meta, and I currently work on agentic post-training data, including the generation and scaling of synthetic reasoning and tool-calling trajectories. I received my Ph.D. in Computational Linguistics from Saarland University.

Research interests: data curation and mixture optimization, data-influence estimation, synthetic data generation, large-scale pretraining and midtraining, on-device and streaming models, multilingual and low-resource NLP.
